Health Flare started as one parent's attempt to make sense of a child's chronic
condition — the flare-ups, the appointments, the patterns that are nearly
impossible to see when you're living inside them day to day. This blog is
where we'll write about building that tool in the open.

## What we'll write about

- **Product decisions** — why the app works the way it does, and the
  trade-offs behind features we ship (or deliberately don't).
- **Privacy engineering** — the practical detail behind "no accounts, no
  servers, nothing leaves your device," not just the marketing line.
- **Dev log** — small, honest updates on progress toward the beta.

We're a small team building something we need ourselves, and we'd rather be
clear about how and why than move fast and stay quiet. If something here
raises a question, [reach out](mailto:hello@healthflare.org) — we'd like to
hear it.
